**New paper.** Following our [theory of appropriateness for generative AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19010), this new [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14050) accounts for norms of rationality. Behavior comes from completing the pattern of "what a person such as I do in a situation such as this," and rationality itself is a culturally contingent standard of justification. Another collaboration led by Joel and Sasha at Google DeepMind.
