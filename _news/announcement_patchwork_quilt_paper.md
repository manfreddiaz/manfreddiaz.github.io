**New Paper.** Following our work on a [theory of appropriateness for generative AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19010), in this new [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05197), we argue that AI safety and alignment should be focused on how humans deal with the irresoluble disagreements that persist in societies and the mechanisms used to prevent them from spiralling into conflict. It is also a [blogpost](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/S8KYwtg5vZqah4SHF/societal-and-technological-progress-as-sewing-an-ever) on LessWrong!
